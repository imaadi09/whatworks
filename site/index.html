Tasks that actually move

Create a task, assign it to anyone in your contacts by phone number, and the conversation lives on the task itself. Status changes, edits and follow-ups all appear in one thread — so context never gets separated from the work.

  • Assign by phone number — no account needed up front
  • BY ME / TO ME split so you see both sides at a glance
  • Full activity log, exportable to CSV or PDF

Know what your team is working on

Open any teammate's view and see the tasks assigned to them and the actions waiting on them. No status meetings, no "did you do that yet?" messages — just the list, live.

  • Assignee tab shows their tasks and pending actions
  • Reminders, follow-ups and escalations in one place
  • Filters for Overdue, Today, This Week, Upcoming

Sales and dashboards, same app

Run enquiries through the same chat and follow-up tools as tasks, and see the numbers that matter on the dashboard — active, overdue, completed this week, completion rate. One app for the work and the scorecard.

  • Sales enquiries with stages and counters
  • Dashboard with active, overdue and pending actions
  • Deadline insights: due today, due this week
