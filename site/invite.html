You have been invited

Someone you work with uses WhatWorks to assign and track jobs, and they have added your number to theirs.

What to do

  1. Install WhatWorks on Android. The Play Store link is coming soon — until then, ask the person who invited you to send you the app file.
  2. Sign in with the mobile number they invited. You will get a one-time password by SMS. There is no password to create and nothing to configure.
  3. Your tasks are already there. Anything assigned to your number before you joined links to your account automatically the first time you sign in.

Use the same number you were invited on. A different number creates a separate account, and the work assigned to you will not appear in it.

Work assigned to you

See what you have been given, what stage it is at, and what is due next.

Follow-ups

Set the next touchpoint and get reminded when it is due.

Joining is free. Premium is an optional paid plan for higher limits, and the person who invited you does not pay for you either way.

Not sure who invited you?

Invites are sent by SMS from the phone of the person inviting you, so the message came from a number you already know. If it does not look familiar, you can ignore it safely — nothing happens until you install the app and sign in yourself. You can also write to support@whatworks.pro.
